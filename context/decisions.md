# Decisions - Task Focuser

| # | Date | Decision | Why | Status |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 001 | 2026-11-04 | Rewrite in .NET 9 WPF (C#), one framework-dependent exe, no NuGet | Native Windows transparency, layered windows, storyboards and a tray icon with no runtime bloat; the .NET 9 desktop runtime is on both machines; Electron would have eaten the resources he asked to spare | accepted |
| 002 | 2026-11-04 | Locked = read-only and draggable, never click-through | His words: dragging stays allowed in lock and double-click unlocks; the old app's click-through lock made the tray the only way out | accepted |
| 003 | 2026-11-04 | Growth anchor is the top-right corner (the note grows left and down), pinned to the work area | His words ("to the left and down"); the right edge is where he taped it | accepted |
| 004 | 2026-11-04 | The state file keeps the old app's path and keys and adds the new ones | His current note text survives the upgrade without a migration step | accepted |
| 005 | 2026-11-04 | Three modes persisted: Note, Dot, Closed; the tray is the only way back from Closed | His words: the dot closes on the bottom-right target and the tray "starts" it again at its original place | accepted |
| 006 | 2026-11-04 | System-DPI aware, not per-monitor | Both his monitors run 125 %; one scale keeps every screen coordinate consistent and the code small | accepted |
| 007 | 2026-11-04 | Light personal project: no skills layer, no inputs | A sticky-note app has no input stream and no project agent to run; the template layer would be dead weight | accepted |
| 008 | 2026-11-04 | The companion is a bird (a parakeet like his Lenny): two legs, a beak, wings as arms; round 2 redraws every frame | His words: "birds have two legs, their beak and their wings are their arms"; the behaviours of round 2 (losing grip, flying back, the chirp) are hers | accepted |
| 009 | 2026-11-04 | Sounds are synthesized in code into WAV files under `%LOCALAPPDATA%\Task Focuser\sounds\` and played through WPF `MediaPlayer`; no audio library | No NuGet (decision 001); a warm two-note chirp needs no samples; MediaPlayer has per-player volume | accepted |
| 010 | 2026-11-04 | The volume slider is piecewise linear: the left half covers 0 to 10 %, the right half 10 to 100 %; default 10 % | His words: the bottom 10 % of the value takes half the bar so the quiet end is fine-tunable; the purpose is a barely audible keep-alive for Bluetooth headphones | accepted |
| 011 | 2026-11-04 | The keep-alive chirp fires every 5 minutes in every mode while the app runs (when sound is on), and the bird flaps with it when it is out | The headphones disconnect while he reads or dictates whatever mode the note is in; "when it's running" | accepted |
| 012 | 2026-11-04 | The bird's window takes clicks on its pixels (no longer click-through) and stays non-activating; dragging the bird drags the note | Clicking the bird and dropping the cucumber on it need hit-testing; a non-activating window never steals his keyboard | accepted |
| 013 | 2026-11-04 | The bar is one row again: cucumber, bird pinned, bird opacity, volume (popup on hover with the idle-sound choices), opacity, colours, `- f +`, plants, lock | His words, the exact order; the second row of round 2 lasted an hour | accepted |
| 014 | 2026-11-04 | Idle sounds come at random intervals from three fuzzy ranges (often 1.5-3 min, mid 3-6, rarely 8-15), chirp or ruffle or both; this replaces the fixed 5-minute keep-alive | His words: ranges, not an exact time; the headphone purpose still holds at "often" and "mid" | accepted |
| 015 | 2026-11-04 | The dot is flat: a disc with a rim and a halo at most 12 %, a slow brightness breath instead of a glow pulse | His words: "flat graphic, almost no glow" | accepted |
| 016 | 2026-11-04 | The vines are procedural pixel art drawn in the note, seeded by the paper size, grown and folded by one animated extent | A decoration that scales with the note and animates as he types, without bitmaps or timers at rest | accepted |
| 017 | 2026-11-04 | `PerchPoint` reports the target layout during the bar's unfold, so the bird flies straight to its final spot | The round-2 teleport came from aiming at the old edge while the note grew left under the bird | accepted |
| 018 | 2026-11-04 | The opacity slider sits after the colour swatches, away from the volume button | His words: not to be confused with the volume icon | accepted |
| 019 | 2026-11-04 | The vines straddle the paper's edge (`EdgeInset`), half inside, half over the transparent stage | His words: it overflows like the bird does into the space around it | accepted |
| 020 | 2026-11-04 | A manual size from the corner grip is a minimum for the auto-size, never a cut: the note still grows with the text; a click on the grip returns to auto | "No scrollbar" (round 1) and "classic resize" (round 4) both hold only if the manual size is a floor | accepted |
| 021 | 2026-11-04 | Typing chains: 20 % per key to start, 2 to 30 pecks, 5 s of silence ends it; the conductor runs it, the bird only pecks | His words; the bird stays dumb about text so its act machine stays small | accepted |
| 022 | 2026-11-04 | Two birds behind one `BirdSkin` contract (23 named frames, a legend, an id); the owl's legend keys are uppercase so one merged brush map serves every sprite | Adding a bird must be art plus sounds only, never behaviour code | accepted |
| 023 | 2026-11-04 | The manual size is honoured only while unlocked; a locked note always compacts | His words: locked = compact, unlocked = the size he set | accepted |
| 024 | 2026-11-04 | The dot remembers its spot: where he last dropped it, carried across monitors as an offset from the work area's top-right corner; the corner only until he moves it | His words: "make the red dot remember its spot"; an offset keeps one habit on both screens | accepted |
| 025 | 2026-11-04 | Public release on GitHub as open source (MIT): the repo `Omni-Wild/task-focuser`, where the v1 Python app stays at tag `v1.0.0`. Published: `app/`, `docs/`, `context/decisions.md`, the README and the LICENSE; the session bookkeeping of the Conflux project folder (`context/state.md`, the plan, the log, the handoffs, `CLAUDE.md`) stays private. Each release carries two exe assets: `TaskFocuser.exe` (self-contained, about 65 MB) and `TaskFocuser-lite.exe` (framework-dependent, about 0.4 MB, needs the .NET 9 or 10 Desktop Runtime). Consequence: every later round is also a release (build both assets, tag `vX.Y.Z`, release notes from SPEC's section for that round); the Conflux folder stays the source of truth and the public repo is a publish target | His friend, and anyone less technical, can download one file and run it, with no SDK, no build and no script | accepted |
